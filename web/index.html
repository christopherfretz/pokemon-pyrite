Saves live in this browser. The in-game save is kept in IndexedDB for this site and is written every 10 seconds and whenever you leave the page. iOS Safari deletes site storage after 7 days without visiting — add this page to your Home Screen and open it regularly, and use Export .sav before a long break. The exported file is raw cartridge RAM: it imports straight into Delta.

Keyboard: arrows move, Z/A = A, X/S = B, Enter = START, Shift = SELECT, hold Space to fast-forward.
